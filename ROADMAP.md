## Priority 1 — HealthKit Historical Data Backfill

**Goal:** On first app launch, pull 30–90 days of historical HealthKit data so new users see a rich health profile immediately rather than starting from scratch.

**Current behavior:** The app only pulls today's steps, the latest single reading for HR/HRV/VO2, and 7 days of sleep. No historical backfill exists.

**Proposed changes:**

- Add a one-time "initial sync" on first launch that queries HealthKit for the past 90 days of data
- Pull daily step totals, heart rate averages, HRV trends, VO2 Max readings, and sleep sessions
- Upload historical snapshots to Supabase in batches
- Show a progress indicator during the initial sync ("Building your health profile...")
- Store a flag in UserDefaults to prevent re-running on subsequent launches

**Files affected:**
- `Services/HealthKitService.swift` — Add historical query methods with date range parameters
- `Services/BackgroundSyncManager.swift` — Add initial sync orchestration
- `ViewModels/DeviceDashboardViewModel.swift` — Update to display historical trend data

## Priority 2 — Provider Web Portal (Clinic Data Entry)

**Goal:** Build a web-based dashboard where providers can log in, select a patient, and enter clinic-collected data and care plans — without needing to call API endpoints directly.

**Current state:** The backend API already supports all the data entry endpoints (testing sessions, care plans, functional assessments, VO2, labs, office measurements, clinician patient list). But there is no front-end UI for providers to use. Currently a provider would have to call the API directly.

**Proposed features:**

- Secure provider login (separate from patient accounts)
- Patient list view with search, showing summary health metrics
- Patient detail view with weekly digest, recent snapshots, and insight history
- Data entry forms for:
  - Testing sessions (initial, quarterly retest, followup)
  - Functional assessments (Sit-to-Stand, Gait Speed, 6-Minute Walk)
  - VO2 Max assessments
  - Lab results (metabolic, lipids, inflammation, hormones)
  - Office measurements (Conneqt, Tanita, grip strength, RMR)
- Care plan builder (workout, nutrition, lifestyle, or combined plans)
  - Set goals with baseline and target values
  - Define weekly schedules (activity, duration, intensity)
  - Add nutrition guidelines and lifestyle notes
- View adherence tracking and progress toward goals

**Tech approach:** Lightweight React web app hosted alongside the existing Node.js backend on Render, or a simple admin panel framework. All data flows through the existing `/clinic/*` and `/clinician/*` API endpoints.

## Priority 3 — AI-Powered Insights and Suggestions

**Goal:** Replace the current rule-based insight engine with AI-generated personalized health suggestions that combine wearable data, clinic data, and care plans to give patients actionable guidance.

**Current state:** Insights are generated by a hardcoded rule engine in `generateInsights.js` that checks thresholds (e.g., "HRV dropped 12%" → generate alert). There is no AI/ML integration of any kind in the codebase.

**Proposed features:**

- Integrate an LLM (Claude API or OpenAI) on the backend to generate personalized health insights
- AI analyzes the full patient context: daily snapshots, trends over time, clinic test results, lab values, current care plan, and adherence data
- Generate natural-language suggestions like:
  - "Your HRV has been trending down over the past 2 weeks. Combined with your elevated resting heart rate, this may indicate increased stress or insufficient recovery. Consider prioritizing sleep and reducing training intensity this week."
  - "Great progress! Your VO2 Max has improved 8% since your last clinic visit. Your cardio plan is working well."
  - "Your lab results show improving inflammation markers. Your current supplement regimen appears to be helping."
- Provider-configurable guardrails (what the AI can and cannot suggest)
- Option for providers to review/approve AI suggestions before they reach patients
- Weekly AI-generated summary emails to patients and/or providers

**Files affected:**
- `services/insights/generateInsights.js` — Replace/augment rule engine with LLM calls
- New: `services/ai/aiInsightsService.js` — AI integration layer
- Backend `.env` — Add AI API key (Anthropic or OpenAI)
- `server.js` — Optional new endpoint for provider to review/approve AI suggestions

## Priority 4 — HIPAA Compliance & Data Encryption Review

**Goal:** Ensure the app meets HIPAA technical safeguard requirements for handling protected health information (PHI), including encryption at rest and in transit, access controls, and audit logging.

**Current state:** The app uses HTTPS/TLS for all API communication (Apple's built-in URLSession/ATS) and Supabase encrypts data at rest on their servers. HealthKit data is encrypted on-device by iOS. However, a formal HIPAA compliance review has not been conducted.

**Action items:**

- Obtain a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with Supabase (available on Pro/Enterprise plans)
- Audit all data flows to confirm PHI is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest (Supabase + device)
- Review any locally cached data beyond HealthKit to ensure it's protected by iOS Data Protection (NSFileProtectionComplete)
- Implement role-based access controls for the provider portal (Priority 2) so only authorized clinicians can view patient data
- Add audit logging for all PHI access (who viewed what, when)
- Evaluate whether additional encryption layers are needed for especially sensitive data (lab results, clinical notes)
- Consult with a healthcare compliance specialist for a formal HIPAA gap analysis
- Document privacy and security policies for the app

**Note:** The App Store encryption export compliance answer ("None of the algorithms mentioned above") remains correct — the app relies on Apple's built-in HTTPS and Supabase's infrastructure encryption, not custom encryption algorithms.

## Priority 6 — Apple Watch App & Complications

**Goal:** Give patients at-a-glance health data on their wrist, keeping Vitaliage top-of-mind and reinforcing daily engagement with their wellness journey.

**Proposed features:**

- Standalone WatchOS companion app showing today's key metrics (steps, resting HR, HRV, sleep score)
- **Watch face complications:**
  - Daily Wellness Score (0–100) — circular gauge complication
  - Today's step count — inline or corner complication
  - Latest resting heart rate — corner complication
  - Daily readiness indicator (green/yellow/red) — small circular complication
- Glanceable "Daily Summary" view in the Smart Stack
- Tap any complication to open the full metric detail on the Watch
- Background refresh to keep complication data current throughout the day
- Sync with the iPhone app via WatchConnectivity framework

**Tech approach:** WatchOS app target added to the existing Xcode project. HealthKit queries run directly on the Watch for real-time data. Complications use WidgetKit (ClockKit deprecated). Care plan and insight data synced from iPhone.

## Priority 7 — Biological Age & Daily Readiness Scores

**Goal:** Provide patients with two signature metrics that make Vitaliage unique — a Biological Age estimate and a Daily Readiness Score — combining wearable data with clinic lab results for a more accurate picture than consumer-only apps.

**Biological Age Score:**

- Calculate estimated biological age by combining HealthKit metrics (VO2 Max, HRV, resting HR, sleep quality, activity levels) with clinic lab values (inflammation markers like hs-CRP, metabolic panel, lipids, hormones)
- Display as a compelling "You're 52, but your body is 45" hero card on the dashboard
- Track biological age over time to show improvement as patients follow their care plans
- Update score when new lab results or clinic assessments are entered
- Research-backed algorithm drawing from established aging clocks (PhenoAge, GrimAge concepts adapted for available biomarkers)

**Daily Readiness Score (0–100):**

- Morning readiness score based on: overnight HRV, resting heart rate, sleep duration and quality, recent activity strain, and HRV trend vs. personal baseline
- Color-coded indicator (green = go hard, yellow = moderate, red = prioritize recovery)
- Factor in clinic data when available (recent lab trends, provider notes)
- Push notification each morning: "Your readiness score today is 82 — great day for your scheduled HIIT session"
- Show on Apple Watch complication (ties into Priority 6)

## Priority 8 — Progress Dashboards & Patient Journey

**Goal:** Visualize the patient's transformation over time, reinforcing the value of the clinic relationship and motivating continued engagement.

**Proposed features:**

- **Before/After dashboard:** Side-by-side comparison of all metrics at first clinic visit vs. current (VO2 Max, body composition, functional assessments, lab values, biological age)
- **Trend charts:** Interactive graphs for each metric over 30/90/180 days with annotations for clinic visits and care plan changes
- **Milestone celebrations:** Achievement badges for goals reached (e.g., "VO2 Max improved 10%", "30-day step streak", "Biological age dropped 2 years")
- **Shareable progress reports:** Generate a PDF or image summary patients can share with family or on social media (with privacy controls)
- **Provider comparison view:** Let clinicians compare a patient's progress against anonymized cohort averages

---

## Priority 9 — Supplement & Medication Tracking

**Goal:** Help patients manage their often complex supplement and medication protocols prescribed by the clinic, with daily reminders and adherence tracking.

**Proposed features:**

- Daily checklist of supplements/medications tied to the patient's care plan
- Push notification reminders at scheduled times
- Adherence tracking with weekly/monthly completion rates
- Provider can prescribe and modify supplement protocols from the web portal
- Flag interactions or conflicts between supplements (basic safety checks)
- Track supplement inventory and remind patients when it's time to reorder

---

## Priority 10 — Secure Messaging with Care Team

**Goal:** Keep patients connected to their care team between clinic visits, reducing no-shows and improving outcomes.

**Proposed features:**

- In-app messaging between patient and assigned clinician
- Support for text, images (e.g., photos of meals, supplement labels), and file attachments
- Provider can send broadcast messages to groups of patients (e.g., clinic announcements)
- Message encryption end-to-end (builds on HIPAA compliance from Priority 4)
- Read receipts and notification badges
- Integration with provider portal (Priority 2) for clinician-side messaging
